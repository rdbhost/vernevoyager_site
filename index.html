We are designing a travel trailer, rethought from the ground up.

Progress

  • March 2026 — Contract signed with Loza and Loza, LLP to file patents on the protectable Intellectual Property (IP) elements within the VV design.
  • July 2024 — Design work is resuming, using Solidworks Simulation Software.
  • November 2023 — Project proponent moved to the Philippines and development was paused for the six month relocation period.
  • September 2020 — Licensed Ansys Engineering Simulation Software under a 3 year Startup license to evaluate design decisions. Prior to this, design work was done using Sketchup and Alibre.
  • Late 2019 — Design initiated, under a new LLC in Texas, Verne Voyager LLC.

Verne Voyager LLC is a Limited Liability Company in the state of Texas.
